How It Works

  1. Open a textChoose a file above. CorpusPrep splits it into sections.
  2. Check the sectionsEach line gets a label. Nothing is taken out yet.
  3. Choose what to keepPick a preset, or tick sections one by one.
  4. Clean and downloadPress Clean text, then save the text and its log.
