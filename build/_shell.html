Prepare a source text for analysis
without losing track of what was removed.

CorpusPrep labels every line of a document before anything is removed. You determine what constitutes your corpus, and the accompanying log documents precisely how that determination was made. Intended for corpus linguists, translation scholars, literary researchers and digital humanists.

Step one
Import
Encoding, byte-order marks and line endings are resolved on import.
Step two
Segment
Every line is labelled: apparatus, front matter, body, back matter.
Step three
Choose
Retain or remove each section. Review the difference before exporting.
Step four
Export
Cleaned text accompanied by a citable log of every decision taken.
